# AI-First Content Governance

## Ownership

- Founder: commercial facts, offer/pricing approval, release cohorts and final claims approval.
- Content reviewer: source relevance, industry specificity, plain-language accuracy and review date.
- Engineering owner: route generation, validation, initial HTML, schema, JSON data, sitemaps and redirects.
- Security/privacy owner: crawler policy, form/API boundaries and public-data review.

## Review cadence

- Quarterly: every industry record and trusted-source URL.
- Immediately: legal entity, registered office, price, guarantee, service scope, regulator guidance or source URL changes.
- 60–90 days after release: pages with zero impressions or poor query fit.
- Before each cohort submission: content, source, canonical, schema and conversion gates.

## Source policy

Use UK government, regulators, official registers, professional bodies, recognised standards and first-party industry sources. A directory or membership supports only the fact it actually records. Secondary commentary cannot be the sole support for regulated or material claims. Record label, HTTPS URL, supported fact, source type and reviewed date.

## Change controls

1. Update the central typed record, not generated HTML or JSON.
2. Preserve the five distinct user intents; do not add query-swap routes.
3. Run all programmatic tests and inspect the 20-pair similarity report.
4. Obtain Founder approval for prices, guarantees, offer names, legal positioning and funnel changes.
5. Update `lastmod` only when substantive reviewed content changes.
6. Submit only approved canonical production URLs after deployment.

## Retirement and redirects

Retire a page only when its buyer need is unsupported, permanently redundant or unsafe to maintain. Map it to the closest genuinely equivalent page with a permanent redirect; do not redirect unrelated pages to the homepage. Remove retired URLs from data indexes, internal links and sitemaps in the same release. Preserve a decision record.

## Prohibited content

- Invented audits, statistics, case studies, testimonials, reviews, awards or accreditations.
- Guaranteed rankings, AI recommendations, leads or regulatory outcomes.
- Competitor claims presented without attributable public evidence.
- City-name or keyword substitutions that do not change the buyer need.
- Hidden content, fake authors, unsupported schema or public personal data.

## Human-value sign-off

For every page the reviewer must answer yes: an owner learns an industry-specific action; the page differs materially from its siblings; it works without the CTA; claims are evidenced or clearly framed as guidance; and Lion Tech Innovations Ltd can responsibly publish it under its legal name.
