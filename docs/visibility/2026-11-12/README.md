# LionTech visibility publication record

We are publishing two original, evidence-led technical articles on our website and maintaining consistent company identity across external profiles.

## Website publication scope

- `/research`: our research and practical guides index.
- `/research/ai-search-business-website-audit`: dated public website observations and source references.
- `/research/payment-fulfilment-eight-test-checklist`: eight local simulation scenarios with reproducible code and results.
- Verified Companies House and LinkedIn company links in our existing Organization schema.
- Native site layout, article metadata, prerendered HTML, internal links and sitemap entries.

## Publication standards

We distinguish our own articles and company profiles from independently accepted editorial coverage. We make no guarantee of indexing, citations, rankings or traffic. All dates and test claims are tied to the accompanying evidence. Our payment simulation makes no network calls and is not a production integration certification.

The OpenAI reference was checked before release and updated to the current crawler documentation. The public audit remains a pre-change baseline; the article explains the identity markup improvement in this release.

## Repository privacy

This is a public-safe edition of our publication pack. Private Search Console records, mailbox identifiers, private correspondence and personal address information are deliberately excluded. The complete original archive remains outside the repository on our authorised desktop.

## Distribution

The profile and social files in this folder are copy assets, not proof of publication. We record verified publication and submission outcomes in `RELEASE-STATUS.md`. External editorial decisions remain with the publishers.
