1/7 Being crawlable, indexed and cited are different things. We checked our own website before publishing more content. Here is the practical audit we used, without ranking promises.

2/7 First, inspect the response. Does the page return HTTP 200? Is the main information in the HTML? Is there a noindex directive? A page loading in your browser is not the same as confirming search-engine inclusion.

3/7 Check robots.txt and hosting access. Allowing a search crawler is an access instruction, not proof that a firewall lets it through or that an answer will cite the page.

4/7 Use Search Console for the indexed state. A live URL test and an indexed URL report answer different questions. Keep those findings separate from traffic and enquiries.

5/7 Check the business identity. Our Organisation markup already had a legal name, website and company identifier. It lacked sameAs links to verified identity pages. Only add profiles that really identify the same business.

6/7 Publish evidence, not invented success stories. Record the date, sampled URLs, method and limits. A directory profile or republished thread is not the same thing as independent editorial coverage.

7/7 Our rule: make the business easy to understand and the evidence easy to inspect. No promised AI rankings. Read our approach: https://liontechinnovations.co.uk/methodology
