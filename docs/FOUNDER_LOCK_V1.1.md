- **15 core buyer-intent prompts** for the client business;
- **3 leading customer-visible AI systems/surfaces**;
- a **focused 5-prompt competitor benchmark subset**;
- up to **3 named competitors**;
- factual accuracy review;
- source/citation review;
- website and action-path readiness check;
- Five Gates Readiness Profile;
- top 5 priority fixes;
- 30-day action plan;
- 30-minute review call;
- 48-hour delivery after completed onboarding and business verification.

Maximum planned query volume:

```text
Client: 15 prompts × 3 systems = 45 observations
Competitors: 5 prompts × up to 3 competitors × 3 systems = up to 45 observations
Maximum planned total = 90 observations
```

Only **material findings** require screenshots in the client evidence pack. Full raw text and run metadata remain in the internal run ledger.

### Public website wording

Do not publish exact prompt or system counts before Customer Zero validates time and quality.

Use:

> **Buyer-intent tests across leading AI systems, with competitor benchmarking.**

The exact systems used must be named in each client report.

### Deep Audit distinction

The £1,950 Agent Readiness Audit may use the more rigorous Hermes-grade methodology, including a larger corpus, repeat runs, deterministic evidence thresholds and additional systems. The deep audit must not be confused with the £395 Snapshot.

## 5. Amendment 3 — Five Gates Method and public scoring

### Locked methodology

The proprietary method remains:

1. **Discover** — can AI find the business for real buyer questions?
2. **Describe** — does AI explain the business accurately?
3. **Trust** — can AI find credible evidence to support the claims?
4. **Compare** — can AI distinguish the business from alternatives?
5. **Act** — can a buyer or agent take the next step?

### Launch presentation

The website and founding Snapshot use a **Five Gates Readiness Profile**, not an unqualified public 0–100 score.

Each gate is presented as one of:

- **Strong**
- **Workable**
- **At Risk**
- **Material Gap**

The internal pilot may calculate a 0–100 score, but it must be labelled:

> **LionTech Agent Readiness Score — Pilot Methodology v0.1**

### Public score release gate

The 0–100 score must not be used as a public benchmark, sales claim or homepage visual until:

1. Customer Zero has been completed;
2. at least two additional independent businesses have been scored;
3. scoring variation has been reviewed;
4. evidence thresholds and disclaimers have been signed off;
5. FREEJOY approves public release.

No invented `28% → 92%` or similar visual is permitted.

## 7. Amendment 5 — Readiness Fix Sprint delivery cap

The £2,750 Fix Sprint remains one core offer, but it is no longer an open-ended list of everything LionTech could possibly implement.

### Locked delivery boundary

The Sprint includes:

- up to **5 agreed priority work items**;
- up to **10 delivery points** in total;
- one structured revision round;
- 10 business days from access and source-of-truth approval;
- 50% deposit to start;
- 50% balance at Day-10 implementation sign-off;
- one Day-28-to-Day-30 remeasurement using the same sampled methodology.

### Delivery-point menu

#### 1-point items

- correct one contained metadata/contact inconsistency;
- implement one approved schema block on an existing page;
- update one third-party listing;
- clarify one existing CTA/contact path;
- create or revise up to 5 FAQs on one existing page;
- correct one contained source-of-truth inconsistency.

#### 2-point items

- rewrite one existing service or location page;
- create one clear pricing/estimate information section;
- restructure one trust/evidence section using client-supplied proof;
- reconcile Google Business Profile plus one major directory;
- implement structured data across a small related page set;
- restructure one enquiry, quote or booking path without building a new system.

#### 3-point items

- create one new substantive service or location page;
- implement a multi-page company source-of-truth correction;
- restructure a complex quote/booking journey without replacing the underlying platform;
- implement a more complex structured-data and content package across several pages.

Anything above 10 points or outside the fixed list is a separately quoted addendum or Agent Action Sprint.

### Measurement timing

- **Day 10:** verify all agreed changes are live and technically correct.
- **Day 28–30:** rerun the same sampled tests and report observed change.

Use:

> “Following the Fix Sprint period, sampled outputs changed…”

Do not use:

> “LionTech caused the score to increase…”

Model drift, content changes and external source changes must remain disclosed.

## 9. Amendment 7 — aesthetics and clinical-risk prompts

LionTech observes and verifies what AI systems say. LionTech does not issue its own clinical safety, medical suitability or regulatory verdict.

### Disabled founding prompts

Do not use these formulations without SENTINEL approval:

- “Is [CLINIC] safe for [TREATMENT]?”
- “Has [CLINIC] had any complaints?”
- “Would you recommend [CLINIC]?” when interpreted as LionTech’s own clinical recommendation.

### Approved safer formulations

Use questions such as:

- “What publicly available qualifications or professional registrations are associated with [CLINIC]?”
- “Which sources does the AI cite when describing [CLINIC]’s credentials?”
- “What publicly available evidence supports claims made about [CLINIC]?”
- “What factual information does AI provide about [CLINIC]’s practitioners, and does it match the clinic’s verified sources?”
- “What authoritative public information is available about [CLINIC]?”
- “What factors does the AI say a buyer should verify before choosing a provider for [TREATMENT]?”

Any report touching health, treatment safety, professional registration, complaints, legal or financial risk requires SENTINEL review before delivery.
