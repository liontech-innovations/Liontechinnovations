## Scope and Turnaround to Accept or Amend

- **Fit:** one opportunity/lot, up to 50 pack pages, fit/mandatory-requirement/evidence-gap triage, concise findings and 20-minute review; no bid drafting. Proposed two working days after accepted scope, complete inputs and cleared payment, subject to confirmed capacity.
- **Standard:** one bidder/opportunity/lot, up to six scored responses, 6,000 final words, 100 pack pages and one consolidated revision. Requirements/evidence mapping, drafting and review. Proposed 5-10 working days from agreed start and complete evidence; agree the actual deadline before a deposit.
- **Complex:** one bidder/defined opportunity/lot, up to 12 scored responses, 12,000 final words, 200 pack pages and two consolidated revisions. Delivery agreed after pack review, not an automatic five-day promise.
- **All:** client supplies and approves technical approach, evidence, commercial pricing and commitments. Exclude award guarantees, default portal submission, legal/tax/engineering advice, fabricated evidence, unlimited revisions and internal-tool access. Larger or multi-lot work is separately scoped.
